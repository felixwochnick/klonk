With the special educational focuses on **language** (*Sprache*), **hearing** (*Hören*), **vision** (*Sehen*), **physical and motor development** (*körperliche und motorische Entwicklung*, KME) and **emotional and social development** (*emotionale und soziale Entwicklung*, ESE), the lower secondary level is taught according to the learning objectives of the Mittelschule. The LehrplanPLUS Förderschule contains separate Computer Science curricula for each focus and grade. They adopt the **learning areas and content of the Mittelschule** (linked there as a "related section from a Mittelschule curriculum") and precede each grade with **development-related competences**.

From grade 7, as at the Mittelschule, there are regular classes and intermediate-certificate classes (*Mittlere-Reife-Klassen*); the M classes require more extensive tasks, a higher pace and more independence.

The following sections describe what the development-related competences of each focus mean for using Klonk.

## Language

In grade 5, the curriculum asks students to find **sensible steps with support** when programming simple processes (e.g. by ordering given sentences, gap texts, multiple choice) and to verbalise their own solutions with **sentence building blocks and phrasing aids**.

- The commands of German Klonk-Script are short German words (`schritt`, `linksDrehen`, `hinlegen`). A word card for each command with a picture of the character secures the technical vocabulary.
- Sentence starters for reflection: "First …, then …, until …" – they correspond directly to the blocks `wiederhole` and `wiederhole solange`.
- [Blocks](/en/klonk-script/blocks) remove the writing hurdle; more confident students can see the corresponding text via **Show Klonk-Script**.

## Hearing

The subject curriculum emphasises focused work in an often noisy learning environment shaped by devices. Klonk gives **no audio feedback** – all information (active line, errors, position of the character) is visible. Error messages appear as text; for students with lower written-language competence they should be read together.

## Physical and motor development

The subject curricula mention adapted hardware and software, choosing the most suitable input method (mouse or keyboard shortcut) and **tracing algorithms in space** (grade 5).

- Blocks are dragged with the mouse; Klonk-Script is written entirely with the keyboard, with suggestions while typing. Depending on motor abilities, one of the two forms may be easier – both produce the same program.
- Single step lets students trace their own program at their own pace.
- Operation with assistive input devices (e.g. eye control, switches) has not yet been tested separately. Please test with your school's equipment before use.

## Emotional and social development

The subject profile emphasises **social learning** and values education. Klonk is suitable for short, clearly defined tasks with immediate, objective feedback: the character shows what the program does – not whether the teacher is satisfied. Pair work with fixed roles (one person dictates, one lays blocks) practises cooperation within a manageable framework. The [learning modules](/en/teaching/modules/overview) with automatic checking provide small successes at short intervals.

## Vision

In addition, grades 2 to 4 have the subject **Information and Communication Technology Education (IKB)** – touch typing and working with screen readers, braille displays and magnification software; programming is not part of it. Computer Science from grade 5 follows the learning areas of the Mittelschule.

Klonk is **visually based**: the 3D world and the blocks require sight.

- **Blind students:** Klonk is currently not suitable. The world has no description for screen readers.
- **Visually impaired students:** use is possible in individual cases – with browser zoom, the dark appearance and Klonk-Script instead of blocks. Please check this in advance with the individual aids. Klonk: ★.
