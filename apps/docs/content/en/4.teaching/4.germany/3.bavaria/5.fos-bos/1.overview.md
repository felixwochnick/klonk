At the Bavarian vocational upper secondary school (*Berufliche Oberschule*) – **Fachoberschule (FOS)** and **Berufsoberschule (BOS)** – computer science teaching depends on the **branch of study** (*Ausbildungsrichtung*). The FOS is attended after the intermediate school-leaving certificate and starts in grade 11 (with practical training, *fachpraktische Ausbildung*); the BOS is attended after vocational training and starts in grade 12. Both lead to the entrance qualification for universities of applied sciences (*Fachhochschulreife*) in grade 12 and to the subject-restricted or general higher education entrance qualification (*fachgebundene/allgemeine Hochschulreife*) in grade 13. A **preparatory class** (*Vorklasse*) may precede them.

## New subjects from 2026/27

The FOS/BOS is currently undergoing a changeover. With the amended timetable (FOBOSO – school regulations for FOS/BOS, Annex 1, valid from 1 August 2026), two new profile subjects are introduced:

- **Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science and Technology** (*Künstliche Intelligenz, Informatik und Technologie*, **KIT**) – branch of study **Technology** (*Technik*); replaces the previous subject *Technologie* there.
- **Artificial Intelligence and Business Informatics** (*Künstliche Intelligenz und Wirtschaftsinformatik*, **KIWI**) – branches of study **Business and Administration** (*Wirtschaft und Verwaltung*) and **International Business** (*Internationale Wirtschaft*), only in grade 12.

The new curricula come into force year by year: preparatory class and grade 11 from **2026/27**, grade 12 from **2027/28**, grade 13 from **2028/29**. Until then, grades 12 and 13 are taught according to the previous curricula (e.g. *Technologie*, *Informatik* as a profile subject in Business and Administration).

In addition, the two-hour **compulsory elective subject Computer Science** (*Wahlpflichtfach Informatik*) continues in grades 12 and 13: deepening the profile in Technology and International Business, broadening the profile in the other branches of study (not in grade 12 of Business and Administration). This subject contains the largest share of programming.

There is no separate subject "Information Technology" at the FOS/BOS – information technology is a learning area within KIT.

**Abbreviations of the branches of study:** T = Technology (*Technik*), ABU = Agriculture, Bio- and Environmental Technology (*Agrarwirtschaft, Bio- und Umwelttechnologie*), W = Business and Administration (*Wirtschaft und Verwaltung*), IW = International Business (*Internationale Wirtschaft*), S = Social Services (*Sozialwesen*), GH = Health (*Gesundheit*), G = Design (*Gestaltung*).

The ABU branch of study also has curricula for KIT 12 and 13; they contain a compulsory learning area "Artificial intelligence and digital applications", but no programming part. For the use of Klonk, the compulsory elective subject Computer Science is the relevant option there.

## Progression with Klonk

1. **Grade 11 (T):** Programming is not yet part of the curriculum – Klonk at most in project work or for binary numbers and coding.
2. **Grade 12 (T):** KIT learning area "Fundamentals of programming" – control structures and test cases in [Python](/en/klonk-script/python) or [Java](/en/klonk-script/java); for groups without prior knowledge with [Klonk-Script](/en/klonk-script/introduction) as an introduction.
3. **Compulsory elective Computer Science (12/13):** software development, procedural and object-oriented programming, relational databases with SQL, IT project – this is where Klonk offers the most opportunities for use.
4. **Grade 13:** object-oriented analysis and projects with several files ([projects](/en/klonk-script/projects)).

Many students come to the FOS/BOS without programming experience (e.g. from the Mittelschule, Realschule or vocational training). The character world makes the effect of control structures visible and can ease the introduction; the switch from Klonk-Script to Python or Java takes place within the same tool.
