In Saxony, the **State Office for Schools and Education** (*Landesamt für Schule und Bildung*, LaSuB) draws up the curricula on behalf of the Saxon State Ministry of Education (*Sächsisches Staatsministerium für Kultus*). All curricula are available in the [curriculum database on the school portal](https://www.schulportal.sachsen.de/lplandb/) (*Lehrplandatenbank im Schulportal*).

Saxon curricula are divided into **learning areas** (*Lernbereiche*) with **guideline times** (*Zeitrichtwerte*, in lessons, *Ustd.*). Per grade, learning areas covering 25 weeks are compulsory; in addition, an **elective area** (*Wahlbereich*, a learning area of an optional nature) covering two weeks can be taught. The learning objectives are graded with fixed terms – *gaining insight* (*Einblick gewinnen*), *knowing* (*Kennen*), *transferring* (*Übertragen*), *mastering* (*Beherrschen*), *applying* (*Anwenden*), *evaluating* (*Beurteilen*), *designing* (*Gestalten*). The pages on the individual grades map Klonk to these learning areas and give an assessment of its suitability for each learning area.

## Computer science as a compulsory subject from grade 7

Since the 2022/23 school year, a new computer science curriculum has been introduced step by step: at the Gymnasium, computer science is a **separate compulsory subject** in grades 7 to 10 with one lesson per week. The curriculum provides for a path from **block-based languages** (grades 7/8) to a **syntax-based programming environment** (grade 10). Klonk covers this transition within one environment: from [blocks](/en/klonk-script/blocks) via [Klonk-Script](/en/klonk-script/introduction) to [Python](/en/klonk-script/python) and [Java](/en/klonk-script/java).

## For school leadership and administration

The Gymnasium computer science curriculum 2022 comes into force grade by grade; it reaches upper secondary with grade 11 from 1 August 2026 and grade 12 from 1 August 2027. In grades 7 to 10, the compulsory subject concerns all students, so a single programming environment across several grades can make coordination within the subject department easier. Klonk runs in the browser without installation. Information on operation, data protection, sign-in and licensing is provided on the page [For schools and school administration](/en/teaching/schools).

For the learning area Algorithms in grades 7 and 8, [learning modules](/en/teaching/modules/overview) with automatically checked tasks are also available; progress is stored only in the students' browser. The grade pages indicate which modules fit.
