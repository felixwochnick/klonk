At the Gymnasium in NRW (G9), computer science is taught as a **compulsory subject in the orientation stage** (*Erprobungsstufe*, grades 5/6), as an **elective subject** (*Wahlpflichtfach*) in grades 9 and 10, and as a subject in **upper secondary** (*gymnasiale Oberstufe*). The following overview shows in which grades Klonk can be used.

| Grade (*Jgst.*) | Subject | Applies to | Klonk | Page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5–6 | Computer science (*Informatik*) | all (compulsory; 2 lessons per week – depending on the school in grades 5 and 6 or only in grade 6) | ★★★ | [Grades 5/6](/en/teaching/germany/north-rhine-westphalia/gymnasium/grade-5-6) |
| 7–8 | no computer science lessons in the timetable | – | optional | – |
| 9–10 | Computer science as an elective subject (stand-alone or combined) | those who choose it | ★★★ | [Grades 9/10](/en/teaching/germany/north-rhine-westphalia/gymnasium/grade-9-10) |
| 11 (EF) | Computer science, introductory phase | those who choose it in upper secondary | ★★★ | [Grade 11](/en/teaching/germany/north-rhine-westphalia/gymnasium/grade-11) |
| 12–13 (Q1, Q2) | Computer science, basic course / advanced course | those who choose it in upper secondary | ★★ | [Grades 12/13](/en/teaching/germany/north-rhine-westphalia/gymnasium/grade-12-13) |

**Abbreviations:** EF = introductory phase (*Einführungsphase*), Q1/Q2 = qualification phase 1/2 (*Qualifikationsphase*), GK/LK = basic/advanced course (*Grund-/Leistungskurs*), WP = elective (*Wahlpflicht*).

## Progression across the grades

For the school-internal curriculum, the following sequence is an option in which students work with the same environment throughout:

1. **Grades 5/6:** Algorithms with sequence, conditional statement and loop – [blocks](/en/klonk-script/blocks) as a visual programming language, custom procedures for modularisation.
2. **Grades 9/10 (WP):** Switching to a text-based language – variables, parameters, syntax and semantics with [Python](/en/klonk-script/python) or [Klonk-Script](/en/klonk-script/introduction).
3. **Grade 11 (EF):** Objects and classes in [Java](/en/klonk-script/java), searching and sorting with piles of bricks.
4. **Grades 12/13 (Q1/Q2):** Recursion, backtracking in a maze, software projects with several files.

Students who did not take computer science as an elective in the middle grades start afresh in the EF; they know the characters and the environment from grades 5/6.

## In grades 7 and 8

The timetable has no computer science lessons in these grades. Klonk can be used in clubs (*Arbeitsgemeinschaften*), supplementary lessons (*Ergänzungsstunden*) or on project days; working with blocks requires no prior knowledge.
