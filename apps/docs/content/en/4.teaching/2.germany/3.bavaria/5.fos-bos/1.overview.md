At the Bavarian vocational upper secondary school (*Berufliche Oberschule*) – **Fachoberschule (FOS)** and **Berufsoberschule (BOS)** – computer science lessons depend on the **branch of study** (*Ausbildungsrichtung*). The FOS is attended after the intermediate school-leaving certificate and starts in grade 11 (with practical training, *fachpraktische Ausbildung*); the BOS is attended after vocational training and starts in grade 12. Both lead to the entrance qualification for universities of applied sciences (*Fachhochschulreife*) in grade 12 and to the subject-restricted or general higher education entrance qualification (*fachgebundene/allgemeine Hochschulreife*) in grade 13. A **preparatory class** (*Vorklasse*) may come before.

## New subjects from 2026/27

The FOS/BOS is currently being restructured. With the amended timetable (FOBOSO – school regulations for FOS/BOS, Annex 1, valid from 1 August 2026), two new profile subjects are introduced:

- **Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science and Technology** (*Künstliche Intelligenz, Informatik und Technologie*, **KIT**) – branch of study **Technology** (*Technik*), replacing the previous subject *Technologie* there.
- **Artificial Intelligence and Business Informatics** (*Künstliche Intelligenz und Wirtschaftsinformatik*, **KIWI**) – branches of study **Business and Administration** (*Wirtschaft und Verwaltung*) and **International Business** (*Internationale Wirtschaft*), only in grade 12.

The new curricula are introduced year by year: preparatory class and grade 11 from **2026/27**, grade 12 from **2027/28**, grade 13 from **2028/29**. Until then, grades 12 and 13 are taught according to the previous curricula (e.g. *Technologie*, *Informatik* as a profile subject in Business and Administration).

In addition, the two-hour **compulsory elective subject Computer Science** (*Wahlpflichtfach Informatik*) remains in grades 12 and 13: deepening the profile in Technology and International Business, broadening the profile in the other branches of study (not in grade 12 of Business and Administration). This is where most of the programming happens.

There is no separate subject "Information Technology" at the FOS/BOS – information technology is a learning area within KIT.

**Abbreviations of the branches of study:** T = Technology (*Technik*), ABU = Agriculture, Bio- and Environmental Technology (*Agrarwirtschaft, Bio- und Umwelttechnologie*), W = Business and Administration (*Wirtschaft und Verwaltung*), IW = International Business (*Internationale Wirtschaft*), S = Social Services (*Sozialwesen*), GH = Health (*Gesundheit*), G = Design (*Gestaltung*).

The ABU branch of study also has curricula for KIT 12 and 13; they contain a compulsory learning area "Artificial intelligence and digital applications", but no programming part – for Klonk, the compulsory elective subject Computer Science remains the option there.

## The common thread with Klonk

1. **Grade 11 (T):** No programming in the curriculum yet – Klonk at most in the project or for binary numbers and coding.
2. **Grade 12 (T):** KIT learning area "Fundamentals of programming" – control structures and test cases in [Python](/en/klonk-script/python) or [Java](/en/klonk-script/java), for learners without prior knowledge with [Klonk-Script](/en/klonk-script/introduction) as an introduction.
3. **Compulsory elective Computer Science (12/13):** software development, procedural and object-oriented programming, IT project – this is where Klonk fits best.
4. **Grade 13:** object-oriented analysis and projects with several files ([projects](/en/klonk-script/projects)).

Many learners come to the FOS/BOS without programming experience (e.g. from the Mittelschule, Realschule or vocational training). The character world makes control structures visible and lowers the barrier to entry – the switch from Klonk-Script to Python or Java is possible without a new tool.
