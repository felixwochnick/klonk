The Gymnasium in Lower Saxony leads to the Abitur in nine years (lower secondary: grades 5–10, introductory phase (*Einführungsphase*): 11, qualification phase (*Qualifikationsphase*): 12 and 13). Computer science is a compulsory subject in grades 9 and 10, is offered in elective or profile courses depending on the school, and in upper secondary.

| Grade (*Jgst.*) | Subject | Applies to | Klonk | Page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5–7 | no computer science lessons in the timetable | – | optional | – |
| 8 | Computer science in elective or profile courses | depending on the school | ★★★ | section “Electives and profile” below |
| 9 | Computer science (compulsory, 1 lesson per week) | all | ★★★ | [Grade 9](/en/teaching/germany/lower-saxony/gymnasium/grade-9) |
| 10 | Computer science (compulsory, 1 lesson per week) | all | ★★★ | [Grade 10](/en/teaching/germany/lower-saxony/gymnasium/grade-10) |
| 11 | Computer science in the introductory phase (2 lessons per week) | choice: three of biology, chemistry, physics, computer science | ★★★ | [Grade 11](/en/teaching/germany/lower-saxony/gymnasium/grade-11) |
| 12–13 | Computer science in the qualification phase (eA/gA) | focus subject, examination subject or supplementary subject | ★★ | [Qualification phase](/en/teaching/germany/lower-saxony/gymnasium/upper-secondary) |

**Abbreviations:** KC = core curriculum (*Kerncurriculum*); eA/gA = advanced/basic level (*erhöhtes/grundlegendes Anforderungsniveau*).

## The learning fields in lower secondary

The [core curriculum for lower secondary](https://cuvo.nibis.de/index.php?p=download&upload=185) arranges the competences in four **learning fields** (*Lernfelder*) with **modules**. It specifies **no numbers of hours** and no assignment to grades – that is set by the school-specific work plan. If a module is included, the competences in the **basic** column (*Basis*) are compulsory; *deepening* (*Vertiefung*) and *supplement* (*Ergänzung*) are added depending on time and learning group.

| Learning field | Module | Klonk |
|---|---|---|
| Data and their traces (*Daten und ihre Spuren*) | Structure of networks with a focus on the internet | – |
| | Data exchange in networks (encoding) | ★ |
| | Data protection and data security | – |
| | Managing data (spreadsheets, databases) | – |
| Computer competence (*Computerkompetenz*) | Structure of computer systems | – |
| | Storing data (files, file types) | ★ |
| | Word processing, presentation, image editing | – |
| Algorithmic problem solving (*Algorithmisches Problemlösen*) | Concept of an algorithm | ★★★ |
| | Algorithmising and implementing | ★★★ |
| Automated processes (*Automatisierte Prozesse*) | automated processes in everyday life | ★ |
| | Modelling automata | ★★ |
| | technical realisation of automated processes (sensors, actuators) | ★ |

Klonk is a **simulation**: the character has sensors (`isWall`, `isBrick`, `isMark`) and actuators (`step`, `placeBrick`, `setMark`), but there is no real hardware. For the module on technical realisation, in which real sensors are read and components are assembled, microcontrollers or robot kits are still needed.

## Electives and profile (grades 8–10)

According to the decree “Work in grades 5 to 10 of the Gymnasium” (*Die Arbeit in den Schuljahrgängen 5 bis 10 des Gymnasiums*), a school can set up a **mathematics/science focus** (*Schwerpunkt Mathematik/Naturwissenschaften*) from grade 8, whose profile lessons may also be used for **computer science**, or offer **elective courses** (*Wahlpflichtkurse*). Whether and to what extent computer science is taught there is up to the school. The basis is the same core curriculum as for the compulsory subject – with more time, the *deepening* and *supplement* columns of the learning field “Algorithmic problem solving” can be fully covered: variables, logical operators, objects with attributes and operations, and a text-based programming language. The ideas on the pages for [grade 9](/en/teaching/germany/lower-saxony/gymnasium/grade-9) and [grade 10](/en/teaching/germany/lower-saxony/gymnasium/grade-10) fit here too, supplemented with [Python](/en/klonk-script/python) or [Java](/en/klonk-script/java).

## The common thread with Klonk

1. **Grade 9:** First algorithms with [blocks](/en/klonk-script/blocks) – sequence, loop, conditional statement.
2. **Grade 10:** Variables, logical operators, testing – with [Klonk-Script](/en/klonk-script/introduction) in German.
3. **Grade 11:** A high-level programming language ([Python](/en/klonk-script/python) or [Java](/en/klonk-script/java)), custom operations, trace tables.
4. **Grades 12/13:** Classes and objects, arrays, recursion (eA), automata and project work.

## In grades 5 to 8

The timetable has no compulsory computer science subject up to grade 8. Klonk is suitable there for clubs (*Arbeitsgemeinschaften*), elective lessons or project days – blocks need no prior knowledge.
