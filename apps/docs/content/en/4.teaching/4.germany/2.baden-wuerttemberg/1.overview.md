In Baden-Württemberg, the **education plan 2016** (*Bildungsplan 2016*) applies, published at [bildungsplaene-bw.de](https://www.bildungsplaene-bw.de). Each subject plan describes **process-related competences** (*prozessbezogene Kompetenzen*, e.g. *modeling and implementing*) and **content-related competences** (*inhaltsbezogene Kompetenzen*), which are divided into numbered areas (e.g. *3.1.2 Algorithms*) and sub-competences (*Teilkompetenzen*). The education plan does not specify hours per area.

## Transition: G9 and the subject "Computer Science and Media Education"

Baden-Württemberg is returning to the nine-year Gymnasium. Since the 2025/2026 school year, Gymnasiums have taught grades 5 and 6 according to the G9 timetable; grade 7 follows in the 2026/2027 school year. The higher grades are still on G8.

G9 introduces the compulsory subject **Computer Science and Media Education** (*Informatik und Medienbildung*) – one lesson per week, continuously from grade 5 up to grade 10 or 11, at all secondary school types. Media education predominates in the lower grades, computer science in the higher ones (including a text-based programming language, algorithms, basics of AI).

| Period | What applies |
|---|---|
| until the 2026/2027 school year | Previous subject plans **Basic Course Media Education** (*Basiskurs Medienbildung*, gr. 5) and **Advanced Course Computer Science** (*Aufbaukurs Informatik*, gr. 7), together with a **reading guide** (*Lesehilfe*) from the Ministry of Education (currently: reading guide for grades 5 to 7, July 2026) |
| from 1 August 2027 (planned) | New subject plan **Computer Science and Media Education**; Basic Course Media Education, Advanced Course Computer Science and the profile subject **Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics** (*Informatik, Mathematik, Physik*, IMP) are phased out |

The reading guide is explicitly **not an enacted education plan**, but an orientation until the new subject plan arrives. The pages on Baden-Württemberg reflect the state as of 2 November 2026 and will be updated as soon as the new subject plan is published.

## School types

| School type (*Schulform*) | Subjects with computer science | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Gymnasium (academic secondary school leading to the Abitur) | Computer Science and Media Education (5–7, G9), profile subject IMP (8–10, G8), Bridge Course Computer Science (*Brückenkurs Informatik*) (9/10), computer science as a basic or advanced subject in the course stage (*Kursstufe*) | [Gymnasium](/en/teaching/germany/baden-wuerttemberg/gymnasium/overview) |

Gemeinschaftsschule (comprehensive school), Realschule (intermediate secondary school) and Werkrealschule (secondary school with a vocational focus) – all covered by the joint education plan for lower secondary (*Gemeinsamer Bildungsplan der Sekundarstufe I*) – as well as the vocational Gymnasiums (*berufliche Gymnasien*) will be added.

## Klonk in relation to the education plan

The education plan does not prescribe a tool. For the introductory stage it names a "suitable, e.g. visual" or "block-based" programming environment, and from the middle grades a textual programming language. Klonk can cover both stages with the same character and the same task world:

- [Blocks](/en/klonk-script/blocks) and [Klonk-Script](/en/klonk-script/introduction) in German for grades 5 to 8
- [Python](/en/klonk-script/python) and [Java](/en/klonk-script/java) for IMP, the bridge course and the course stage
- Single step, breakpoints and the variable view in all languages – this allows the *step-by-step examination of code* required by the education plan to be implemented directly

## For school leadership and administration

With the introduction of Computer Science and Media Education in G9 and the new subject plan planned for 1 August 2027, schools need a programming environment that extends from the block-based introduction in grade 5 to a textual language in the higher grades. Klonk runs in the browser without installation and can be used consistently across these grades. Information on operation, data protection, sign-in and licensing is provided on the page [For schools and school administration](/en/teaching/schools).

For the introduction to programming in grades 5 to 7, [learning modules](/en/teaching/modules/overview) with automatically checked tasks are also available; progress is stored only in the students' browser. The grade pages indicate which modules fit which grade.
