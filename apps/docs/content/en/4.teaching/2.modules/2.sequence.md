**Module 1** · Control Klonk with single commands – one instruction after the other.

In the app under **Learn** (`/learn/sequence`). The tasks can be solved with blocks or in Klonk-Script and are checked automatically.

## Learning objectives

- Understand a program as a sequence of instructions
- Use the commands `step`, `turnLeft`, `turnRight`, `placeBrick`, `pickBrick` and `setMark` confidently
- Plan the path of a character ahead and check it step by step

## Introduction for students

This is how the introduction appears in the app:

A program is a sequence of **instructions**. Klonk carries them out in order – from top to bottom, each exactly once.

```klonk
step
step
turnLeft
placeBrick
setMark
```

- `step` – one cell forward (`step(3)` moves three cells)
- `turnLeft` / `turnRight` – a quarter turn
- `placeBrick` – place a brick on the cell **in front of** Klonk, `pickBrick` takes it away again
- `setMark` – mark the cell **under** Klonk

With **Step** (F10) your program runs line by line. That way you can see exactly what each instruction does.

## Tasks

### 1. To the mark

Klonk should stop exactly on the yellow mark.

- **Checked:** final position of the character – in one start world
- **Hints in the app:** Count the cells up to the mark. Each `step` moves Klonk one cell further.

**Sample solution**

```klonk
step
step
step
step
```

### 2. Around the corner

The way to the mark leads around a corner. Bring Klonk to the yellow mark – without walking into a block.

- **Checked:** final position of the character – in one start world
- **Hints in the app:** Klonk faces east (right). Think about whether Klonk has to turn left or right at the corner – from its own point of view.

**Sample solution**

```klonk
step
step
turnRight
step
step
step
```

### 3. A small wall

Build a wall of three bricks to the left of Klonk's path – on the three cells next to the yellow marks. It does not matter where Klonk ends up.

- **Checked:** bricks and marks of all cells – in one start world
- **Hints in the app:** `placeBrick` puts the brick in front of Klonk. So Klonk first has to turn towards the wall and then back again.

**Sample solution**

```klonk
step
turnLeft
placeBrick
turnRight
step
turnLeft
placeBrick
turnRight
step
turnLeft
placeBrick
```

### 4. Tidying up

Two bricks lie on Klonk's path. Pick up both, walk to the end of the path and mark the cell there.

- **Checked:** final position of the character, bricks and marks of all cells – in one start world
- **Hints in the app:** `pickBrick` takes the brick **in front of** Klonk. So place Klonk directly in front of the brick. · Klonk can also climb onto a single brick – but then the brick stays where it is.

**Sample solution**

```klonk
step
pickBrick
step
step
pickBrick
step
step
setMark
```

## Notes for teachers

**Time needed:** about one double lesson.

**Typical difficulties:** Left and right from the character's point of view (not the viewer's) – rotating the world or acting out the movement helps. `placeBrick` and `pickBrick` affect the cell **in front of** the character, `setMark` the cell **under** it.

**Differentiation:** Students without prior experience start with blocks and use “Show code” to see the generated Klonk-Script. Fast learners solve task 3 with as few instructions as possible and compare their solutions.

**Transition:** The long sequences in tasks 3 and 4 motivate the next module (repetition).
