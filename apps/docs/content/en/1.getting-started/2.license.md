Klonk is **open core** with a split license (overview in the `LICENSE` file in the repository):

| Part | License |
|---|---|
| `packages/klonk-script` (Klonk-Script) and `packages/world` (world model) | [MIT](https://opensource.org/license/mit) – free to use, also in your own projects |
| Platform: app, admin, sign-in, docs, `core`, `ui`, `runtime`, `csharp` | [Elastic License 2.0](https://www.elastic.co/licensing/elastic-license) |

## What the Elastic License 2.0 allows

- **Self-hosting** Klonk – for your own school, company or privately.
- Reading, modifying and redistributing the source code (with notice of your changes).

## What it prohibits

- Providing Klonk to **third parties as a hosted service**.
- Circumventing, removing or changing the **license token check** or unlocking the functionality it protects.

Full functionality requires a [license token](/en/administration/license). Only the license text is legally binding.

## Third-party software

Klonk uses many open-source libraries – including Nuxt, Monaco, Blockly, three.js, Pyodide, Jedi, TeaVM and Roslyn. The complete list with licenses is in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` in the repository.
