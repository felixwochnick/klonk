In Bavaria, the **LehrplanPLUS** of the State Institute for School Quality and Educational Research (*Staatsinstitut für Schulqualität und Bildungsforschung*, ISB) applies, published at [lehrplanplus.bayern.de](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de). It divides each subject into **learning areas** (*Lernbereiche*) with **competence expectations** (*Kompetenzerwartungen*). The following pages map Klonk to these learning areas for each school type and grade.

## School types

Depending on the school type, computer science content in Bavaria is part of a combined subject, a subject in its own right or a modular subject:

| School type (*Schulform*) | Subjects with computer science | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Gymnasium | Science and Technology (*Natur und Technik*) (6, 7), Computer Science (*Informatik*) (NTG from 9), late-starting computer science (*spät beginnende Informatik*) (11, 12), upper school (*Oberstufe*) (12, 13) | [Gymnasium](/en/teaching/germany/bavaria/gymnasium/overview) |
| Realschule | Information Technology (*Informationstechnologie*, IT, modular): foundation course (*Anfangsunterricht*, 5–8), advanced course (*Aufbauunterricht*) depending on the elective subject group (*Wahlpflichtfächergruppe*) | [Realschule](/en/teaching/germany/bavaria/realschule/overview) |
| Mittelschule | Computer Science (compulsory, 5–10), elective subject Computer Science and Digital Design (*Informatik und digitales Gestalten*, 7–10) | [Mittelschule](/en/teaching/germany/bavaria/mittelschule/overview) |
| FOS/BOS | KIT – Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science and Technology (*Künstliche Intelligenz, Informatik und Technologie*, 11–13, Technology), KIWI (12), compulsory elective subject Computer Science (12, 13) | [FOS/BOS](/en/teaching/germany/bavaria/fos-bos/overview) |

**Gymnasium** = academic secondary school up to the general higher education entrance qualification (*Abitur*); **Realschule** = secondary school for grades 5–10 with the intermediate school-leaving certificate; **Mittelschule** = secondary school for grades 5–9/10 with a strong vocational orientation; **FOS/BOS** = vocational upper secondary schools (*Fachoberschule*/*Berufsoberschule*) for grades 11–13.

## Robot Karol and Klonk

Many Bavarian schools use Robot Karol to introduce algorithms. Klonk takes up the same idea – a character that places bricks, sets marks and detects walls – and extends it:

- 3D world with several characters and custom designs
- Klonk-Script in German **and** English, plus blocks
- the same path continues all the way to Java, Python and C# – with a debugger, in the browser, without installation

Teachers who already work with Robot Karol can therefore largely carry over existing task ideas and continue in higher grades with the same environment.

## For school leadership and administration

The place of computer science differs considerably between school types: the Gymnasium follows the curriculum for the nine-year Gymnasium (G9), computer science is a compulsory subject in grades 5 to 10 at the Mittelschule, and at FOS/BOS the new subjects KIT and KIWI are being introduced year by year from the 2026/27 school year. Klonk mainly covers the learning areas on algorithms and programming, not the subject as a whole. Questions on operation, data protection, sign-in and licensing are addressed on the page [For schools and school administration](/en/teaching/schools).

For the introduction to programming – for example at the Gymnasium in grade 7, in module 1.2 at the Realschule and in the compulsory subject at the Mittelschule – [learning modules](/en/teaching/modules/overview) with automatically checked tasks are also available; progress is stored only in the students' browser. The grade pages indicate which modules fit.
