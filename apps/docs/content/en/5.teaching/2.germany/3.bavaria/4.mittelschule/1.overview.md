The **Mittelschule** is a secondary school type for grades 5 to 9 (or 10), leading to the first school-leaving certificate and, in the M stream, to the intermediate certificate (*Mittlere Reife*). At the Bavarian Mittelschule, **Computer Science** (*Informatik*) **is a compulsory subject of its own** – continuously from grade 5 to 10, with **one lesson per week** in each grade according to the timetable (*Stundentafel*). Every subject curriculum from grade 5 onwards contains a learning area **Programming** (*Programmieren*); this makes the Mittelschule one of the school types where Klonk fits continuously for the longest time.

From grade 7, the curriculum comes in two versions: for **regular classes** (*Regelklassen*, R) and for **intermediate certificate classes** (*Mittlere-Reife-Klassen*, M, "M stream"). The learning areas are the same; the M version demands more independence, more extensive tasks and a faster pace. Grade 10 exists only as an intermediate certificate class.

## What about Economics and Communication, Technology, Science and Technology?

At the Mittelschule, programming appears **only in the subject Computer Science** (and in the elective subject). The other subjects with a "computer" component have different focuses:

- **Economics and Communication** (*Wirtschaft und Kommunikation*, WiK), career-oriented compulsory elective subject: word processing, layout, image editing, presentation, spreadsheets, internet applications, touch typing – using software, no algorithms. Klonk: –.
- **Technology** (*Technik*), career-oriented compulsory elective subject: circuits with sensors in grade 9, mechatronics in M10. There, learners are to control or regulate a technical system with the computer, "simulated if necessary" – Klonk can serve as a simulation to prepare *how* a program decides based on sensor values (`isWall`, `isBrick`), but does not replace real hardware. Klonk: ★.
- **Science and Technology** (*Natur und Technik*): science subject (including sensors in technology in grade 9), without programming. Klonk: –.

## The common thread with Klonk

1. **Grade 5:** breaking processes down into sub-steps – sequence and repetition with a fixed count with [blocks](/en/klonk-script/blocks).
2. **Grade 6:** condition, alternative and conditional repetition – the character reacts to its world.
3. **Grade 7:** variables and methods – custom procedures in [Klonk-Script](/en/klonk-script/introduction).
4. **Grades 8 and 9:** functions with return values, output of data, sub-methods – small games and projects, from grade 9 preferably in [Python](/en/klonk-script/python).
5. **Elective subject:** object-oriented programming with custom classes in [Java](/en/klonk-script/java) or Python, software projects in teams.

## What Klonk does not cover at the Mittelschule

In every grade, the curricula also require content that Klonk is not intended for: file system, internet services and data protection, image and graphics editing, spreadsheets (grades 8 and 9), networks (grade 9) and databases (grade 10). Other tools are needed for these.
