title: Private accounts & operators
description: Sign in without a school or company – with Klonk ID or common providers.
---

Anyone who doesn't belong to a school or company signs in with a **private account**. The buttons only appear on the app's sign-in page for providers that are configured.

## Klonk ID (Authentik)

A platform-wide OpenID Connect provider such as Authentik. It serves two purposes:

- **Private accounts** for all users of that provider
- **Operator access**: members of the groups in `NUXT_PLATFORM_OIDC_OWNER_GROUPS` (owner) or `NUXT_PLATFORM_OIDC_DEVELOPER_GROUPS` (developer) manage all organizations in the admin area. The role is taken from the groups on every sign-in – removing someone from the group revokes their rights on the next sign-in.

| Variable | Description |
|---|---|
| `NUXT_PLATFORM_OIDC_ISSUER` | e.g. `https://auth.example.org/application/o/klonk/` |
| `NUXT_PLATFORM_OIDC_CLIENT_ID` / `_CLIENT_SECRET` | Application credentials |
| `NUXT_PLATFORM_OIDC_NAME` | Display name, default “Klonk ID” |
| `NUXT_PLATFORM_OIDC_GROUPS_CLAIM` | Claim containing the groups, default `groups` |

Redirect URIs: `<app-url>/auth/oidc/callback` and `<admin-url>/auth/oidc/callback`.

## Google, Microsoft, Apple, GitHub

Credentials via `NUXT_OAUTH_<PROVIDER>_CLIENT_ID` and `_CLIENT_SECRET` (Apple: `TEAM_ID`, `KEY_ID`, `PRIVATE_KEY`). Callback URL: `<app-url>/auth/<provider>`, e.g. `https://app.klonk.example/auth/github`. Accounts are recognized only by the provider ID, never by e-mail address.
