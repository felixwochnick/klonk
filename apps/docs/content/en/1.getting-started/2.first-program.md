## 1. Sign in and open

Open Klonk in your browser and sign in – with your school or company account (`name@domain`) or a private account. You land in **Code**: the code editor on the left, the world with a character on the right.

## 2. Choose a language

Choose the language at the top right of the editor. **Blocks** or **Klonk-Script** are a good start.

## 3. Write a program

Type in Klonk-Script:

```klonk
step
placeBrick(red)
turnLeft
step
setMark
```

With blocks, drag the same commands from the **Movement** and **Bricks & marks** categories into the workspace.

## 4. Run

Click **Run**. The character takes a step, places a red brick in front of it, turns left, walks on and marks its field. **Reset** restores the world.

::tip
**Step** (F10) runs the program one command at a time – so you see exactly what each line does. Clicking left of a line number sets a breakpoint.
::
