At the Gymnasium in Baden-Württemberg, two programs run side by side in the 2026/2027 school year: grades 5 to 7 already learn in **G9** with the new compulsory subject **Computer Science and Media Education** (*Informatik und Medienbildung*), grades 8 to 12 are still in **G8** under the education plan 2016 (*Bildungsplan 2016*). Without the IMP profile and without the bridge course, there are no computer science lessons in grades 8 to 10; Klonk can be used there in extracurricular clubs and on project days. For the course stage there is also the plan **Computer Science, elective subject in the upper school** (*Informatik, Wahlfach in der Oberstufe*, pilot program).

## Subject names in Baden-Württemberg

- **Computer Science and Media Education** (*Informatik und Medienbildung*) – new compulsory subject in G9, one lesson per week from grade 5. Until the new subject plan (planned for the 2027/2028 school year), it is taught according to the previous plans *Basic Course Media Education* (*Basiskurs Medienbildung*) and *Advanced Course Computer Science* (*Aufbaukurs Informatik*), supplemented by the **reading guide Computer Science and Media Education for grades 5 to 7** (*Lesehilfe Informatik und Medienbildung für die Klassen 5 bis 7*, July 2026).
- **Advanced Course Computer Science** (*Aufbaukurs Informatik*) – previous one-lesson compulsory subject in grade 7 (G8); in G9 the basis for grade 7 until the new subject plan applies.
- **Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics (IMP)** (*Informatik, Mathematik, Physik*) – profile subject in grades 8 to 10 (G8); according to the education plan, the computer science part comprises a total of 4 annual weekly hours (*Jahreswochenstunden*). Phased out with the new education plan.
- **Bridge Course Computer Science** (*Brückenkurs Informatik*) – for students without IMP who want to take computer science in the course stage: 2 annual weekly hours (one lesson per week in 9 and 10, or two lessons per week in 10).
- **Computer science in the course stage** (*Kursstufe*) – basic subject (*Basisfach*) or advanced subject (*Leistungsfach*); the subject plan is published as *Computer Science (pilot program)* (*Informatik (Schulversuch)*).

## Progression across the grades

For the subject department, the following progression is one option; the world, the character and the commands stay the same, while the language and the level of demand increase:

1. **Grades 5/6:** First steps – statements and loops with a fixed count in [blocks](/en/klonk-script/blocks).
2. **Grade 7:** All basic building blocks with conditions, variables and values – blocks and [Klonk-Script](/en/klonk-script/introduction); the reading guide explicitly names the "movement path of a figure" as an example.
3. **Grade 8 (IMP):** Procedures, random numbers, logical operators and test cases – still in Klonk-Script.
4. **Grade 9 (IMP/bridge course):** Switch to a textual language ([Python](/en/klonk-script/python) or [Java](/en/klonk-script/java)) with arrays, data types and the debugger.
5. **Grade 10:** Two-dimensional arrays – the world as a grid and a pixel image.
6. **Course stage:** Object orientation, stack, recursion (Towers of Hanoi), backtracking and projects.
