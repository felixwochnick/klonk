**Module 2** · Instead of copying the same instructions, let them repeat.

In the app under **Learn** (`/learn/repeat`). The tasks can be solved with blocks or in Klonk-Script and are checked automatically.

## Learning objectives

- Recognize recurring sequences of instructions
- Use the counting loop `repeat … times`
- Nest loops

## Introduction for students

This is how the introduction appears in the app:

If instructions repeat, you do not have to copy them. `repeat … times` carries out everything up to `end` as often as you specify:

```klonk
repeat 4 times
  step
  placeBrick
end
```

The indented lines form the **loop body**. A loop body may contain another loop – then the inner loop runs completely in each pass of the outer one.

## Tasks

### 1. Long wall

Build a wall of eight bricks to the left of Klonk's path – on the cells next to the yellow marks. Use a loop.

- **Checked:** bricks and marks of all cells – in one start world
- **Requires:** counting loop (`repeat … times`)
- **Hints in the app:** First write down what Klonk has to do for **one** brick. These instructions go into the loop.

**Sample solution**

```klonk
repeat 8 times
  turnLeft
  placeBrick
  turnRight
  step
end
```

### 2. A lap of the square

Klonk walks once around the square and marks each of the four corners. In the end Klonk stands at the start again, facing the same direction as at the beginning.

- **Checked:** final position of the character, direction it faces, bricks and marks of all cells – in one start world
- **Requires:** counting loop (`repeat … times`)
- **Hints in the app:** One side of the square is three steps long. What repeats four times?

**Sample solution**

```klonk
repeat 4 times
  setMark
  step(3)
  turnRight
end
```

### 3. Three towers

Build a tower of four bricks on each cell with a yellow mark. Klonk cannot climb over a tower – it has to walk around it (via the top row).

- **Checked:** bricks and marks of all cells – in one start world
- **Requires:** counting loop (`repeat … times`)
- **Hints in the app:** A tower is a loop itself: `placeBrick` four times. · After the tower, Klonk must stand exactly as before the first tower – just two cells further to the right.

**Sample solution**

```klonk
repeat 3 times
  repeat 4 times
    placeBrick
  end
  turnLeft
  step
  turnRight
  step(2)
  turnRight
  step
  turnLeft
end
```

## Notes for teachers

**Time needed:** about one double lesson.

**Typical difficulties:** What belongs in the loop body, what before or after it? A good approach is to first write down the sequence for one pass (e.g. in task 1: turn, place, turn back, step) and then put it into the loop. Often the last turn is missing, so the character faces the wrong way after the first pass.

**Differentiation:** Task 3 (nested loops) is demanding and can serve as an extra task. Step mode shows well how often the inner loop runs.

**Assessment:** Suitable are rewriting a long sequence into the shortest possible loop solution, or a structure chart for a given solution.
