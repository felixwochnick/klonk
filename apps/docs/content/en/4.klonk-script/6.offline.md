Klonk is an **installable web app (PWA)**. In Chrome and Edge, **Install app** appears at the top right. After that, Klonk starts in its own window like a regular program.

## Working offline

On the first visit, the browser stores the whole app: editor, blocks, 3D world and characters. After that, **Code**, **World** and **Projects** also work without internet. A notice **Offline** then appears at the top.

- Programs run locally in the browser anyway, so they work offline too.
- Browser projects and project folders live on your device and stay available offline.
- **Signing in requires a connection.** Once signed in, you can keep working offline. Signing out ends that.
- The app fetches new versions automatically as soon as you are back online.
