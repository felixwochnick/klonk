**Subject:** Computer Science (*Informatik*) · **Applies to:** special schools with the focus on learning (*Förderschwerpunkt Lernen*), lower secondary level (compulsory subject, 1 lesson per week) · [Full curriculum text](https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/fachlehrplan/foerderschule/5/informatik/foerderschwerpunkt/lernen)

The subject curriculum *Informatik 5–9* is **not divided into grades**: the competences describe the result of a five-year learning process. The teacher selects which competences a student aims for on the basis of the initial learning situation and individual support needs. The content is taken from the Mittelschule curriculum or the school's internal curriculum. According to the subject profile, programming starts "playfully" from grade 5; in grades 7 to 9 the focus is on application software.

## 4 Programming

Each learning area begins with **development-related competences**, followed by the subject competences.

**4.1 Development-related competences (summary):** perceive and carry out processes in small steps · imitate steps, put them in the right order and verbalise them · break situations down into steps and represent them enactively, iconically and symbolically · choose and try out strategies with given aids (e.g. **programming blocks**) and take feedback on board · check results and correct mistakes independently · deal constructively with programming errors · solve problems with algorithms together in a group · experience that different solutions can lead to the goal.

**4.2 Processes and algorithms (summary):** observe everyday processes, describe them with the properties of an algorithm (precise, finite, effective, elementary) and break them down into steps · recognise the need for clear instructions in **role play (human robot)** · with guidance, **create objects** in a suitable (also analogue) development environment and change them with simple algorithmic building blocks · use **algorithmic structures and variables** largely independently · test and improve simple programs.

### Using Klonk

In several respects, Klonk matches the curriculum word for word: the role play "human robot" is followed by the character on screen with the same commands; the [blocks](/en/klonk-script/blocks) are the programming blocks named in the curriculum; with `erstelle` (`create`), students create their own characters (objects) and change the world with `schritt`, `hinlegen` and `markieren`. Because the curriculum spans five years, the same environment can be used from grade 5 to 9 – with a growing share of control structures.

### Lesson sequence (suggestion over several years)

| Stage | Focus | Task in Klonk |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Role play human robot | Without a computer: one child is the robot and does exactly what is said, nothing more. What happens with "go over there"? ⇒ instructions must be **precise** and **elementary**. |
| 2 | Instruction, sequence | Guide the character to a mark – first with arrow cards on the table (iconic), then with blocks |
| 3 | Finding mistakes | The teacher provides a program with one mistake; the class runs it in **single step** and finds the wrong command |
| 4 | Repetition with a fixed count | A wall of five bricks: first `hinlegen` five times, then with `wiederhole 5 mal` |
| 5 | Objects | Create a second character with `erstelle("Kim")`, switch between characters with `wechsle` – each character has a name, a position and a viewing direction |
| 6 | Condition | `wiederhole solange nicht istWand` – the character walks to the wall, however large the world is |
| 7 | Variables | Count how many bricks have been placed and display the number with `ausgabe` |

```klonk
// Walk to the wall, mark every cell blue, the last one green
repeat while not isWall
  setMark(blue)
  step
end
setMark(green)
```

In German classes: `wiederhole solange nicht istWand`, `markieren(blau)`, `schritt`, `ende`, `markieren(grün)`.

::tip
Before starting, have the class predict how many cells will turn blue – and then run the same task in a larger world. The program does not need to be changed. A second solution with `repeat 4 times` only works in a world of a particular size: students experience that **different solutions can lead to the goal**, but not all of them are equally general.
::

### Suitable learning modules

The [learning modules](/en/teaching/modules/overview) are suitable for students who can already work independently. The task texts are written for the Mittelschule; for the focus on learning they should be read aloud, discussed together or prepared with picture cards. The automatic check gives immediate feedback without the teacher having to look at every solution.

- **First steps: instructions** and **Repetition with a fixed count** – stages 2 to 4
- **Conditional repetition** and **Branching** – stage 6
- **Variables and counting** – stage 7

### Planning notes

- **Practise often, repeat regularly** (subject profile): many short tasks in the same world rather than one long one. A world with several marks allows tasks such as "to the yellow mark", "to the red mark".
- **Verbalising:** have students read their program aloud ("The character takes two steps, turns left …"). This corresponds to the competences in *communication and language*.
- **Pair work:** one person calls out, the other lays the blocks; then they swap (competence *ability to cooperate*).
- **Documentation:** the [structogram](/en/klonk-script/diagrams#structogram) generated by Klonk can be printed and stuck into the exercise book – an iconic representation of one's own program.
- **Ergonomics:** the development-related competences also mention an ergonomic sitting position – pay attention to it during longer programming phases.

## Elective Computer Science and Digital Design

Special schools with the focus on learning can offer the elective *Computer Science and Digital Design* (*Informatik und digitales Gestalten*, 2 lessons per week) in grades 7 to 9. It follows the modular curriculum of the Mittelschule; notes on Klonk are on the page [Elective Computer Science and Digital Design](/en/teaching/germany/bavaria/mittelschule/informatik-digitales-gestalten).
