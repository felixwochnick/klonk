At the Saxon Gymnasium, computer science is a **separate compulsory subject in grades 7 to 10** (one lesson per week each). In grades 5 and 6, the timetable includes the subject **Technology/Computers** (*Technik/Computer*); in upper secondary (grades 11/12, *Jahrgangsstufen*), there is a **basic course** and – at a few schools – an **advanced course** in computer science.

The basis is the **Gymnasium computer science curriculum 2022** (*Lehrplan Gymnasium Informatik 2022*, [full text, PDF](https://www.schulportal.sachsen.de/lplandb/lehrplan/file/630/fTqTlkXseYZLUMB5l3pV)). It has been phased in since 2022: grade 7 from 2022, grade 10 from 2025, **grade 11 from 1 August 2026**, grade 12 from 1 August 2027. In the 2026/27 school year, grade 12 is therefore still taught according to the previous curriculum.

**Abbreviations:** LB = learning area (*Lernbereich*), Ustd. = lessons (*Unterrichtsstunden*), Gk/Lk = basic/advanced course (*Grund-/Leistungskurs*).

**Upper secondary:** Any school can offer the computer science basic course; it can replace biology, chemistry or physics, or civics/law/economics (*Gemeinschaftskunde/Rechtserziehung/Wirtschaft*) or geography. According to the school regulations, the computer science **advanced course** is offered at the three M.IT. Gymnasiums (Gymnasium Dresden-Pieschen, Karl-Schmidt-Rottluff-Gymnasium Chemnitz, Gerda-Taro-Schule Leipzig) and in the specialised mathematics and science programme (*vertiefte mathematisch-naturwissenschaftliche Ausbildung*). Special rules apply to these schools when the curriculum comes into force.

## Progression across the grades

For coordination within the subject department, the following sequence lends itself:

1. **Grade 7:** First algorithms in a block-based language – [blocks](/en/klonk-script/blocks) in Klonk, the character as an object with attributes and methods.
2. **Grade 8:** Mastering sequence, conditional statement, counting loop and conditional loop – blocks and [Klonk-Script](/en/klonk-script/introduction) side by side.
3. **Grade 9:** No programming in the compulsory learning areas (databases, AI, networks); in the elective area *graphs*, the world serves as a maze.
4. **Grade 10:** Switching to a syntax-based language – [Python](/en/klonk-script/python) or [Java](/en/klonk-script/java) with data types, combined conditions and subroutines; the complex task (*Komplexaufgabe*) as a project.
5. **Grades 11/12:** Object orientation, recursion, search and sorting methods, in the advanced course data structures and backtracking.

## In grades 5/6

In learning area 1, Technology/Computers covers *basics of using digital media* (files, word processing, research) – programming is not part of the compulsory content. The elective area *IPO principle with single-board computers* (*EVA-Prinzip bei Einplatinenrechnern*) targets real hardware with sensors and actuators, which Klonk does not replace. For clubs (*Arbeitsgemeinschaften*), project days or a playful preview of grade 7, [blocks](/en/klonk-script/blocks) can nevertheless be used.
