In Saxony, the **State Office for Schools and Education** (*Landesamt für Schule und Bildung*, LaSuB) draws up the curricula on behalf of the Saxon State Ministry of Education (*Sächsisches Staatsministerium für Kultus*). All curricula are available in the [curriculum database on the school portal](https://www.schulportal.sachsen.de/lplandb/) (*Lehrplandatenbank im Schulportal*).

Saxon curricula are divided into **learning areas** (*Lernbereiche*) with **guideline times** (*Zeitrichtwerte*, in lessons, *Ustd.*). Per grade, learning areas covering 25 weeks are compulsory; in addition, an **elective area** (*Wahlbereich*, a learning area of an optional nature) covering two weeks can be taught. The learning objectives are graded with fixed terms – *gaining insight* (*Einblick gewinnen*), *knowing* (*Kennen*), *transferring* (*Übertragen*), *mastering* (*Beherrschen*), *applying* (*Anwenden*), *evaluating* (*Beurteilen*), *designing* (*Gestalten*).

The *Oberschule*, special schools (*Förderschulen*) and the vocational Gymnasium (*Berufliches Gymnasium*) will follow.

## Computer science from grade 7 – for everyone

Since the 2022/23 school year, a new computer science curriculum has been introduced step by step: at the Gymnasium, computer science is a **separate compulsory subject** in grades 7 to 10 with one lesson per week. The path leads from **block-based languages** (grades 7/8) to a **syntax-based programming environment** (grade 10) – exactly the transition that Klonk covers from [blocks](/en/klonk-script/blocks) via [Klonk-Script](/en/klonk-script/introduction) to [Python](/en/klonk-script/python) and [Java](/en/klonk-script/java).
