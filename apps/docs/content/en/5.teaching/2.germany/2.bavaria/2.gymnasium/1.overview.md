At the Bavarian Gymnasium (G9), computer science is taught in different subjects. Which of them a class has depends on the **branch of study** (*Ausbildungsrichtung*).

| Grade (*Jgst.*) | Subject | Applies to | Klonk | Page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Science and Technology (*Natur und Technik*, without a computer science part) | – | optional | – |
| 6 | Science and Technology – focus on computer science (*Natur und Technik – Schwerpunkt Informatik*) | all | ★★ | [Grade 6](/en/teaching/germany/bavaria/gymnasium/grade-6) |
| 7 | Science and Technology – focus on computer science | all | ★★★ | [Grade 7](/en/teaching/germany/bavaria/gymnasium/grade-7) |
| 8 | no computer science lessons | – | optional | – |
| 9 | Computer Science (*Informatik*) | NTG | ★★★ | [Grade 9](/en/teaching/germany/bavaria/gymnasium/grade-9) |
| 10 | Computer Science | NTG | ★★★ | [Grade 10](/en/teaching/germany/bavaria/gymnasium/grade-10) |
| 11 | Computer Science / late-starting computer science (*spät beginnende Informatik*) | NTG / HG, SG, MuG, SWG | ★★★ | [Grade 11](/en/teaching/germany/bavaria/gymnasium/grade-11) |
| 12 | Computer Science (eA, gA), late-starting computer science (gA) | upper school (*Oberstufe*) | ★★ | [Grade 12](/en/teaching/germany/bavaria/gymnasium/grade-12) |
| 13 | Computer Science (eA, gA) | upper school | ★ | [Grade 13](/en/teaching/germany/bavaria/gymnasium/grade-13) |

**Abbreviations:** NTG = science and technology branch (*naturwissenschaftlich-technologisches Gymnasium*), HG = humanities branch (*humanistisches*), SG = languages branch (*sprachliches*), MuG = arts and music branch (*musisches*), SWG = economics and social sciences branch (*wirtschafts- und sozialwissenschaftliches Gymnasium*); eA/gA = advanced/basic level (*erhöhtes/grundlegendes Anforderungsniveau*).

## The common thread with Klonk

1. **Grade 7:** algorithms with control structures – blocks or Klonk-Script in German.
2. **Grade 9 (NTG):** object orientation – the same character becomes the object `new Klonk()` in Java or Python.
3. **Grade 10 (NTG):** arrays, references and polymorphism with several characters and custom classes.
4. **Grade 11:** algorithmics for late-starting computer science, graph search in the NTG – the world becomes a maze.
5. **Grade 12:** recursion and depth-first search, software projects with several files.

Schools without an NTG branch pick up again after grade 7 in grade 11 with late-starting computer science – using the same tools.

## In grades 5 and 8

Neither grade (*Jahrgangsstufe*) has computer science lessons in the curriculum. Klonk is suitable there for elective courses, project days or cover lessons – blocks need no prior knowledge.
