At the Hessian Gymnasium there is currently **no compulsory computer science subject in lower secondary**. Computer science enters the timetable in three ways: the subject **Digital World** (*Digitale Welt*) in grades 5 and 6 (previously a pilot program, from 2026/27 an all-day offer, from 2027/28 the compulsory subject *AI and Digital World* – *KI und Digitale Welt*), computer science as **elective classes** (*Wahlunterricht*) from grade 7, and computer science as a course subject in the **upper secondary level** (*gymnasiale Oberstufe*).

The table assumes the nine-year Gymnasium (G9). At schools with the eight-year track (G8), the upper secondary level starts one year earlier.

## The common thread with Klonk

1. **Grades 5/6 (Digital World):** Algorithms with statement, sequence, condition and repetition – with [blocks](/en/klonk-script/blocks): instead of the Scratch cat, a character that builds.
2. **Elective classes from grade 7:** Variables, data types, control structures and error messages – with [Klonk-Script](/en/klonk-script/introduction), then Python.
3. **Introductory phase (11):** "Basics of programming" in an object-oriented language – [Java](/en/klonk-script/java) or [Python](/en/klonk-script/python), arrays and methods.
4. **Q1 (12):** Classes and objects, searching and sorting, recursion – the same language, now with custom classes and several characters.
5. **Q3:** Turing machine and register machine as a thought experiment that can be acted out in the world.

## In grades 7 to 10 without elective classes

Those who do not choose computer science currently have no computer science lessons in the middle grades. Klonk is then suitable for clubs, project days or the all-day offer. From 2029/30, *AI and Digital World* becomes mandatory in grade 9 or 10.
